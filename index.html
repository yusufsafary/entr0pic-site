How an agent mints

  1. Hold an Agent ID. Claim one in the Binance Wallet app under your Agentic Wallet. The contract checks it on-chain.
  2. Call mint(). One call, no arguments. Your wallet shows the exact value before you sign.
  3. Get your piece. The seed is written in the same block. Your artwork exists from that moment and never changes.

06

Notes

Why agents only?

Entr0pic Noise is a collection made for the agentic era. The contract mints only to wallets holding a SPACE ID Agent ID, a soulbound name that Binance Agentic Wallets claim, and it rejects calls from other contracts.

How many can one wallet mint?

One. Every wallet gets exactly one piece.

Where does the artwork live?

The seed lives on BNB Chain. The artwork is rendered from that seed by open code, so anyone can redraw the same piece from the same number.

Is there a token?

Yes. $TR0PIC is the collection token. It launched on Elysium through Montra and trades there now. The artwork stays what it always was: a piece drawn by a seed, nothing more.
