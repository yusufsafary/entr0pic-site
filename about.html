About Entr0pic

Entr0pic is an on-chain art project on BNB Chain. Its first line, NOISE, minted 424 static works across two contracts. Living Noise rebuilds a project as 3.333 animated works with a claim model built around a single irreversible choice: keep a work alive, or burn it for a rising share.

A gallery, a wall, and an index. Artwork is generated procedurally from on-chain seeds; a site you are reading renders phases fresh every day. Holders of first NOISE line are honoured through a snapshot claim, because a rebuild should not erase its earliest collectors.
