Whitepaper

Living Noise, in full

Entr0pic began as NOISE, a static collection. Living Noise is a rebuild on BNB Chain with a single idea: a work should not sit still, and holding it should force a real choice.

Living works

Each of 3.333 works is an animated GIF drawn procedurally from its own on-chain seed: flowing signal filaments over obsidian, a pulsing core, a 2.4 second loop. On this site a work changes phase every day at 00:00 UTC, so a gallery never looks quite same twice. Works 1 to 20 may sit in one wallet; a first work is free for Agent ID holders who answer a wallet-bound question, and paid tiers rise gently from 0.0002 BNB.

Two Doors

Every work can claim $TR0PIC exactly once, through exactly one door. KEEP pays 250.000 $TR0PIC and a work lives on. COLLAPSE burns a work permanently and pays a Collapse Index, a tiered amount driven by remaining supply at a moment of burning: 750.000 early, rising to 2.500.000 among last 500. Scarcity is not a promise here; it is arithmetic. Every collapse makes remaining works rarer and next collapse richer, while shrinking a pool of future keepers. A Living Wall keeps burned works visible as ash tiles, so a collection remembers what it spent.

Legacy snapshot

Original NOISE works, Nº 1 to 424 across two first contracts, were frozen into a Merkle snapshot at rebuild. Each snapshot work claims 250.000 $TR0PIC once, with no fee, and is never burned. Works minted by old contracts after a snapshot hold no claim right.

Token

$TR0PIC supply is fixed at 10.000.000.000, minted once, with no tax. A pool opens on Uniswap on Friday, 20 November 2026 from a 1.000.000.000 liquidity reserve.

Risks

Smart contracts are audited by reading: all four Living Noise contracts are verified on Sourcify and linked in a footer. Token value after launch is set by a market, not by this paper. See Disclaimer.
